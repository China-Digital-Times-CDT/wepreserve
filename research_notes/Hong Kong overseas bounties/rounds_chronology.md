# Hong Kong national-security arrest warrants and police bounties against people living outside Hong Kong: round-by-round chronology (July 2023 to 20 October 2026)

**Coverage date:** Research done on 2026-10-20. The **primary roster** is the Hong Kong Police Force's official machine-readable wanted-persons feed for the National Security Department, downloaded on 2026-10-20 ([EN feed](https://www.police.gov.hk/info/appeals_public/nsc/en.xml); [TC feed](https://www.police.gov.hk/info/appeals_public/nsc/tc.xml)). The feed lists **34 cases**. Its most recent revision is dated **2026-07-25**, and no case ID from 2026 appears. The latest news items found are dated 6 October 2026 ([Hong Kong Watch](https://www.hongkongwatch.org/all-posts/2026/10/6/new-us-bill-would-offer-permanent-residence-to-hong-kongers-as-arrests-of-ded-holders-continue)) and 20 October 2026 ([Vision Times](https://www.visiontimes.com/2026/10/20/joe-tay-paul-chiang-face-off-at-the-ballot-box-after-2025-foreign-interference-case.html)).

**Method caveats:**
- HKFP blocked every direct fetch with HTTP 429, and the Wayback Machine returned 403. HKFP facts below therefore come from search-result snippets of HKFP headlines and ledes. They are flagged "(HKFP, snippet)".
- AP, Reuters, BBC and Guardian could not be searched with domain filters because of crawler blocks. AP wire copy was read through syndicating outlets (cfjctoday, abc17news/CNN, PBS, NBC, US News, philstar).
- Official wording comes from info.gov.hk and police.gov.hk. It is cited as the government's own claims.
- Safety: only the country of residence as publicly reported is given. Dates of birth and "date of leaving Hong Kong" appear in the police feed but are deliberately omitted. Relatives are not named, except where a relative was publicly prosecuted.

## Q1. Round 1: 3 July 2023 (eight people, HK$1 million each)

### Takeaway
Verified. On 3 July 2023 the National Security Department (NSD) put eight overseas activists on its wanted list. A magistrate had issued arrest warrants, and police offered a HK$1 million reward per person. The eight were Kevin Yam, Elmer Yuen, Anna Kwok, Dennis Kwok, Ted Hui, Mung Siu-tat, Finn Lau and Nathan Law. They lived in the US, UK and Australia. John Lee said they would be "pursued for life" unless they surrendered, and called them "street rats". **Running total: 8.**

### Cited Findings
- Police press release, 3 July 2023, "Persons wanted for alleged contravention of Hong Kong National Security Law". It names YAM Kevin, YUAN Gong-yi, KWOK Fung-yee, KWOK Wing-hang, HUI Chi-fung, MUNG Siu-tat, LAU Cho-dik and LAW Kwun-chung. The reward is "$1 million in respect of each wanted person". Offences, collectively: incitement to secession, subversion, incitement to subversion, and collusion with a foreign country or external elements. It cites NSL Arts 37–38 on extraterritorial reach and says "Endangering national security is a very serious offence." — [info.gov.hk P2023070300526](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202307/03/P2023070300526.htm); [news.gov.hk "8 wanted for NSL offences"](https://www.news.gov.hk/eng/2023/07/20230703/20230703_154532_575.html)

## Q3. Agnes Chow (周庭): what is documented

### Takeaway
Police declared Agnes Chow "wanted" on 6 February 2024 for failing to report on police bail and said she would be "pursued for life". **No bounty was ever announced for her.** She is **not** in the police NSD reward feed as of 2026-10-20, and she was not named in any of the four bounty rounds or the three absconder-specification rounds found.

### Cited Findings

## Q5. Round 4: 25 July 2025, the "Hong Kong Parliament" (19 warrants; 15 new bounties at HK$200,000)

### Takeaway
Verified. On 25 July 2025 police announced court warrants for **19 people** on suspicion of **subversion (NSL Art. 22)** over the overseas "Hong Kong Parliament".
- **Four** were already on the list at HK$1 million: Elmer Yuen, Victor Ho, Johnny Fok and Tony Choi.
- **15 new names** got the first-ever lower tier of **HK$200,000**: 5 alleged organisers and 10 elected "members".
- On 4 August 2025, 16 of the 19 were specified as absconders.

**Running total: 34 people with outstanding bounties** (19 at HK$1 million and 15 at HK$200,000).

### Cited Findings
- Police release, 25 July 2025: 19 wanted for allegedly organising, establishing or taking part in the "Hong Kong Parliament".
  - Nine organisers: Yuan Gong-yi, Ho Leung-mau Victor, Fok Ka-chi, Choi Ming-da, Chan Lai-chun, Feng Chongyi, Gong Sasha, Ng Man-yan and Tsang Wai-fan.
  - Ten candidates sworn in as "members": Chin Po-fun, Ha Hoi-chun Paul, Hau Chung-yu, Ho Wing-yau, Keung Ka-wai, Lam Tony, Ng Agnes, Wong Chun-wah, Wong Sau-wo and Zhang Xinyan.
  - Rewards: HK$1M each for the first four and HK$200,000 each for the other 15.
  - Rhetoric: "endangering national security is a very serious offence". Police cited NSL Arts 37–38 (extraterritoriality) and Art. 33 (lighter penalty for surrender), and warned that aiding or funding participants, including online, is illegal. The investigation continues and more people may be added.
