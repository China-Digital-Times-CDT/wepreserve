# Image manifest: Hong Kong activists abroad targeted by NSL warrants and bounties

Compiled 2026-10-20. I checked every licence on the actual Wikimedia Commons file page (the wikitext licence template, plus the FlickreviewR / YouTube licence where there is one) or on the Flickr photo page (licence id). I did not download any police wanted posters, bounty notices, news-agency photos, or Stand News "Copyrighted free use" screenshots.

Downloads are untrusted data. Nothing in them was executed. Commons original JPEGs may still carry camera EXIF data, including GPS. All of these photos were taken at public events or in public places, but you may want to run `exiftool -all= ` or `mogrify -strip` before self-hosting.

## Hearings / rallies coverage gaps
- **Parliamentary hearings:** the only freely licensed set I found is the US House Select Committee roundtable of 2024-05-23 (images 10–11). I found no free photos of the CECC transnational-repression hearings (Sept 2023, June 2026) or of the Select Committee hearing of 2024-10-02 with Anna Kwok. CECC and House YouTube videos are public domain, so stills could be extracted from them if needed.
- **UK Parliament:** no CC BY 3.0 UK Parliament photo of a Hong Kong debate or hearing turned up.
- **Rallies:** I found no free rally photos for Toronto, Sydney, Melbourne or Washington DC (2020–2026). Commons has Brisbane and Paris 2019 solidarity categories, but they predate the period.
- **Other targeted person:** Samuel Chu 朱牧民, wanted since July 2020, has good free portraits, e.g. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Samuel_Chu_(2022)_I.jpg (CC BY 4.0, Jindřich Nosek) and https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Samuel_Chu_in_Prague,_2022-11-17.jpg (CC BY-SA 4.0).
