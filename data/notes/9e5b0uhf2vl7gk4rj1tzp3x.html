🔥 Hong Kong Fire 2025

Photo: Tyrone Siu/Reuters.

A catastrophic fire at Wang Fuk Court in Tai Po, Hong Kong, engulfed seven of eight residential towers on November 26, 2025 — the city's deadliest fire since 1948. Authorities cite gross negligence in renovation works as a key driver of the blaze and its rapid spread. This hub is the entry point; the detail lives on the pages linked below.

At a glance

The fire26 Nov 2025, Wang Fuk Court, Tai Po. 168 dead, 79 injured; 7 of 8 towers burned — Hong Kong's deadliest fire since 1948.
CauseCigarettes in an air shaft most likely ignited non-compliant scaffold netting and foam boards; lead counsel told the inquiry that nearly all fire-safety systems failed "because of human factors". → Investigation
The inquiryJudge-led Independent Committee (David Lok) — 80 witnesses, 30 sessions; lead counsel's closing address said the government bore "unshirkable responsibility." Report submitted 22 Oct 2026.
The reportRedacted version published 29 Oct 2026 — certain parts redacted to avoid prejudicing the criminal prosecutions. → The Report & the Reckoning
AccountabilityCE John Lee and seven officials bowed in apology; Housing chief Winnie Ho resigned; the three other ministers over faulted departments are staying. Bereaved residents want all four out, and about 40–50 civil servants face a three-month investigation. → The Report & the Reckoning
Criminal case7 people + 2 firms (Prestige Construction, Will Power Architects) face 25 charges incl. manslaughter; next hearing 14 Jan 2027.
ReformsCriminalise bid-rigging, scrap and replace the ICU, a new Building Maintenance authority, and a Buildings Ordinance overhaul.
ResettlementHK$6.8b buyback; 99.1% accepted, 17 holdouts; a months-long fight for a homeowners' meeting. → Resettlement & Owners

Key Facts

  • Impact and scale: Fire began ~2:52 p.m. on Nov 26, 2025 at Wang Fuk Court, Tai Po (~2,000 flats/~4,600 residents; ~40% aged 65+). Seven of eight towers burned; 200 fire trucks, 100 ambulances, and >800 firefighters/paramedics deployed. Building fatalities: Wang Tai 82, Wang Cheong 70, Wang Sun 3, Wang Do 2, Wang Shing 1; none in Wang Yan/Wang Kin/Wang Chi; one victim not yet tied to a block (UDN/Ming Pao/TVBS/DotDotNews). SPCA estimates >537 animals affected; 364 found (294 survived, 70 died, 173 missing) (RTHK).
  • Materials and systems: Highly flammable polystyrene and foam panels blocked windows and escape routes; alarms found non-functional across all towers (some residents say alarms were switched off during works). Officials say 7 of 20 scaffold-net samples failed fire-resistance tests; allegations of mesh swapping to evade inspections (AP). Elevator-lobby windows reportedly sealed with foam boards; residents complained in 2024 about flammable netting/foam (NYT). PolyU’s Xinyan Huang later clarified remarks on bamboo’s role and urged caution pending a full technical study.
