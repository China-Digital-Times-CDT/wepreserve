On **26 November 2025**, a fire at Wang Fuk Court in Tai Po killed **168 people** — Hong Kong's deadliest fire since 1948. This page exists to hold their names.

## Who they were

The dead were **58 men and 110 women**, aged from **six months to 98 years**. Among them were **one firefighter** (senior fireman Ho Wai-ho), **two renovation workers**, **five construction workers**, and **ten foreign domestic helpers** — women from Indonesia and the Philippines who died alongside the families they worked for. **Thirty-seven households lost at least two members.** By 15 January 2026 all 168 had been identified through forensic work, and every missing-person case was resolved.

## The names were almost never told

For months after the fire the government **did not release the victims' names**. On 15 January 2026 the Secretary for Security said the names would be withheld "to respect the wishes of the bereaved"; Ming Pao reported that some families had not been asked whether they consented. The names entered the public record only on **10 June 2026**, when prosecutors charged seven people and two firms over the deaths and **read all 168 names into the court record** in the charge sheet. We set them down here so that they are remembered as people, not as a number.

## About this list

The names are given as they appear in the **prosecution's charge sheet**, read in open court on 10 June 2026 — the first time the full list became public. Because the names were first transcribed by journalists from that record, we cross-checked them across three independent transcriptions (HK01, Sing Tao, and the record kept on Chinese Wikipedia) and reconciled the few differences; a reputable source copy is preserved in the archive. We have deliberately **not** relied on state-media versions. The names are shown in the order of the court record. If you spot an error in a name — the thing that matters most on a page like this — please [tell us](https://github.com/China-Digital-Times-CDT/wepreserve/issues/new?template=04-bug-report.md) so we can correct it.
