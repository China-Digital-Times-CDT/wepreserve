## Q1. 〈自己的文章〉 "Writing of One's Own" (1944), Chang's rebuttal to Fu Lei: exact wording

### Takeaway
All the requested passages are confirmed verbatim in at least two independent texts, and the essay is the clearest first-hand statement of Chang's humanist poetics. Its claims are:
- the 安穩 ("stable, secure") side of life is the 底子 ("ground") of the 飛揚 ("soaring") side, and has 永恆的意味 ("something of the eternal");
- her characters are "weak ordinary people" (軟弱的凡人) and "the broad bearers of this age" (這時代的廣大的負荷者);
- she writes "only small matters between men and women" and not "monuments of the era".

There are real textual variants between the 1944 first printing, the Crown text and mainland texts. The most important are 不澈底 vs 不徹底, and 不完全 vs 不安全.

### Cited Findings

**Bibliography.** The essay was first published in 《新東方雜誌》, vol. 9, nos. 4–5 combined issue, May 1944, as a direct reply to 迅雨 (Fu Lei)'s 〈論張愛玲的小說〉 in 《萬象》, vol. 3 no. 11, May 1944 — [SSQ](https://www.chinawriter.com.cn/n1/2020/0909/c419384-31855145.html). It was collected in 《流言》 (Shanghai, December 1944) — [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/021.htm). In the Crown 《流言》 (1973) the passages below fall on pp. 21–23 — [GYW](https://scholars.lib.ntu.edu.tw/bitstreams/aea4a80b-cdea-43cd-8b85-afa4211beeb2/download).
- [SSQ] adds that the line about not attempting a "monument of the era" (時代的紀念碑) answers Hu Lancheng's 〈皂隶·清客與來者〉 in 《新東方雜誌》 1944 no. 3, while Fu Lei is the main addressee.
- The often-repeated claim that the essay was **also reprinted in Hu Lancheng's 《苦竹》** (1944) is UNVERIFIED in these notes.

**English translations that exist.**
- Andrew F. Jones (trans.), "Writing of One's Own", in *Written on Water* (Columbia UP, 2005), pp. 15–22 — [KL](https://hkupress.hku.hk/image/catalog/pdf-preview/9789888083725.pdf), notes citing "Writing of One's Own," *Written on Water* 15–22, and pp. 16, 17, 18, 19.
- Leo Ou-fan Lee translated excerpts in *Shanghai Modern* (Harvard UP, 1999), p. 282 — [USC/Bernards slide](https://libraries.usc.edu/sites/default/files/events/documents/Bernards%20-%20Eileen%20Chang%20Centennial%20Presentation.pdf).
- I could not see Jones's exact wording for the 安穩/飛揚 passage.

**(a) 飛揚 vs 安穩: "the stable side is the ground" (41 characters). Grade V2+.**
「我發現弄文學的人向來是注重人生飛揚的一面，而忽視人生安穩的一面。其實，後者正是前者的底子。」
- Matched in [TP](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_28451163) and [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/021.htm).
- The next sentences are: 「又如，他們多是注重人生的鬥爭，而忽略和諧的一面。其實，人是為了要求和諧的一面才鬥爭的。」
- Fresh rendering: "I find that people who write literature have always stressed the soaring, exalted side of life and neglected its stable, secure side. In fact the latter is the ground of the former."

**(b) "Something of the eternal" (100 characters). Grade V2+ except one variant.**
「強調人生飛揚的一面，多少有點超人的氣質。超人是生在一個時代裡的。而人生安穩的一面則有著永恆的意味，雖然這種安穩常是**不完全**的，而且每隔多少時候就要破壞一次，但仍然是永恆的。它存在於一切時代。它是人的神性，也可以說是婦人性。」
- [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/021.htm) reads **不完全的** ("incomplete"). [TP](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_28451163) reads **不安全的** ("insecure"). Everything else matches. The variant is **unresolved**; I lean towards 不完全, but this must be checked against Crown 《流言》 p. 20–21 before quoting.
- Fresh rendering: "To stress the soaring side of life has something of the Superman about it. The Superman is born into one particular era. But the stable side of life has something of the eternal. Though this stability is often incomplete, and every so often is bound to be destroyed, it is still eternal. It exists in every era. It is humanity's divinity — or one could call it womanliness."
- The same "Superman vs. divinity/womanliness" pairing recurs in 〈談女人〉 (see Q4), where Chang says divinity is 「廣大的同情，慈悲，了解，安息」. This is Chang's own, first-hand vocabulary of compassion.

**(c) "Without this ground, soaring is only froth" (73 characters to 浮沫). Grade V2+.**
「文學史上素樸地歌詠人生的安穩的作品很少，倒是強調人生的飛揚的作品多，但好的作品，還是在於它是以人生的安穩做底子來描寫人生的飛揚的。沒有這底子，飛揚只能是浮沫，許多強有力的作品只予人以興奮，不能予人以啟示，就是失敗在不知道把握這底子。」
- Matched in [TP](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_28451163), [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/021.htm) and the HK [EDB PDF](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/tc/curriculum-development/kla/chi-edu/resources/lit-teacher-training/20251013_Clit_1.pdf). The EDB PDF prints 在于, a simplified-form leak.

**(d) 壯烈 / 悲壯 / 蒼涼, and 「蔥綠配桃紅」「參差的對照」 vs 「強烈的對照」 (93 characters). Grade V2+.**
「我不喜歡壯烈。我是喜歡悲壯，更喜歡蒼涼。壯烈只有力，沒有美，似乎缺少人性。悲壯則如大紅大綠的配色，是一種強烈的對照。但它的刺激性還是大於啟發性。蒼涼之所以有更深長的回味，就因為它像蔥綠配桃紅，是一種參差的對照。」
- Variants:
  - [TP](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_28451163) has 缺乏人性 where [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/021.htm) has 缺少人性.
  - [MB] misprints 悲劇則如 and 回昧. [TP] reads 悲壯則如, which fits the sense.
  - Check 缺少/缺乏 against Crown.
- 「蔥綠配桃紅，是一種參差的對照」 is also quoted on an Eslite (誠品) Crown book page, via a search result only.
- Fresh rendering: "I don't like the heroically tragic [壯烈]. I like the tragic-and-stirring [悲壯], and even more the desolate [蒼涼]. The heroically tragic has only force and no beauty; it seems to lack humanity. The tragic-and-stirring is like a colour scheme of bright red and bright green, a strong contrast; but it excites more than it enlightens. The desolate has a deeper, longer aftertaste because it is like onion-green set against peach-pink: an uneven, irregular contrast."
- 參差的對照 has been rendered "equivocal contrast" ([Sydney Review of Books](https://sydneyreviewofbooks.com/essay/eileen-chang/)) and "contrast in de-cadence" (Fiss, [USC](https://libraries.usc.edu/sites/default/files/events/documents/Ge%CC%81raldine%20Fiss%20Response%20to%20Presentations_v2.pdf)). Lee leaves it as *cenci de duizhao*.

**(e) 「時代是這麼沉重」 and 「這時代的廣大的負荷者」 (150 characters; the 負荷者 sentence alone is 18). Grade V2+, with significant variants.**
1944 first printing as quoted by [SSQ](https://www.chinawriter.com.cn/n1/2020/0909/c419384-31855145.html), converted to traditional:
「極端病態與極端覺悟的人究竟不多。時代是這麼沉重，不容那麼容易就大澈大悟。這些年來，人類倒底也這麼生活了下來，可見瘋狂是瘋狂，還是有分寸的。所以我的小說裡，除了《金鎖記》裡的曹七巧，全是些不澈底的人物。他們不是英雄，他們可是這時代的廣大的負荷者。因為他們雖然不澈底，但究竟是認真的。他們沒有悲壯，只有蒼涼。悲壯是一種完成，而蒼涼則是一種啟示。」
- The Crown 1973 text, quoted by [GYW](https://scholars.lib.ntu.edu.tw/bitstreams/aea4a80b-cdea-43cd-8b85-afa4211beeb2/download) at p. 21, also reads **不澈底**. It runs 「我的小說裡，除了〈金鎖記〉裡的曹七巧，全是些不澈底的人物。他們不是英雄，他們可是這時代的廣大的負荷者。因為他們雖然不澈底，但究竟是認真的。他們沒有悲壯，只有蒼涼。悲壯是一種完成，而蒼涼則是一種啟示。」
- Mainland texts differ:
  - [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/021.htm) normalises to 不徹底 and 大徹大悟, and reads 人類到底.
  - [TP](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_28451163) reads 不那么容易 (dropping 容), and runs 「他們也只能如此極端病態」 together without the full stop.
- **Use 不澈底 (the 1944 and Crown reading).** The fuller version above, from 極端病態 onwards, rests on [SSQ] plus [MB]. [MB] also has 不容那麼容易.
- Published translations:
  - Unattributed (SRB essay, author not captured): "Times as weighty as these do not allow for easy enlightenment. … They are not heroes, but they are of the majority who actually bear the weight of the times. As equivocal as they may be, they are also in earnest about their lives. They lack tragedy; all they have is desolation. Tragedy is a kind of closure, while desolation is a form of revelation." — [Sydney Review of Books](https://sydneyreviewofbooks.com/essay/eileen-chang/)
  - Fiss quoting "My Own Writing", translator not named: "My characters are not heroes, but they carry the general burden of this age … Tragic grandeur is a form of closure, but desolation is revelation" — [USC/Fiss](https://libraries.usc.edu/sites/default/files/events/documents/Ge%CC%81raldine%20Fiss%20Response%20to%20Presentations_v2.pdf)

**(f) 「軟弱的凡人」 (44 characters). Grade V2+.**
「我知道我的作品裡缺少力，但既然是個寫小說的，就只能盡量表現小說裡人物的力，不能代替他們創造出力來。而且我相信，他們雖然不過是軟弱的凡人，不及英雄的有力，但正是這些凡人比英雄更能代表這時代的總量。」
- Matched in [TP](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_28451163) and [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/021.htm).
- The 1944 printing per [SSQ](https://www.chinawriter.com.cn/n1/2020/0909/c419384-31855145.html) reads **不及英雄有力**, without 的.
- Published English, apparently Jones (*Written on Water*): "Although they are merely weak and ordinary people and cannot aspire to heroic feats of strength, it is precisely these ordinary people who can serve more accurately than heroes as a measure of the times." — quoted by Shu-Ling Chua, [AAWW](https://aaww.org/a-lifelong-romance-reflections-on-eileen-changs-life-work-and-legacy/). AAWW's bibliography lists Jones's *Written on Water*, but the quotation itself is not page-cited, so the attribution to Jones is probable but unconfirmed.
- Another English version, translator not named: "it is precisely these average people who can better represent, more so than heroes, the sum total of this age." — [USC/Fiss](https://libraries.usc.edu/sites/default/files/events/documents/Ge%CC%81raldine%20Fiss%20Response%20to%20Presentations_v2.pdf)

**(g) 「時代的紀念碑」 and 「我甚至只是寫些男女間的小事情」 (103 characters to 放恣的). Grade V2+.**
「一般所說『時代的紀念碑』那樣的作品，我是寫不出來的，也不打算嘗試，因為現在似乎還沒有這樣集中的客觀題材。我甚至只是寫些男女間的小事情，我的作品裡沒有戰爭，也沒有革命。我以為人在戀愛的時候，是比在戰爭或革命的時候更素樸，也更放恣的。」
- Matched in [TP](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_28451163) and [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/021.htm). [MB] has OCR errors (索樸, 放您) that are corrected here from [TP].
- The two key phrases are also quoted by [SSQ](https://www.chinawriter.com.cn/n1/2020/0909/c419384-31855145.html) from the 1944 text.
- Published English, unattributed: "people are more straight-forward and unguarded in love than they are in war or revolution" and "A real revolution or revolutionary war … should be as emotionally unguarded and as able to penetrate into every aspect of one's life as romantic love" — [Sydney Review of Books](https://sydneyreviewofbooks.com/essay/eileen-chang/)

**(h) Truth within falsehood (48 characters). Grade V2+.**
「只是我不把虛偽與真實寫成強烈的對照，卻是用參差的對照的手法寫出現代人的虛偽之中有真實，浮華之中有素樸」
- Matched in [GYW](https://scholars.lib.ntu.edu.tw/bitstreams/aea4a80b-cdea-43cd-8b85-afa4211beeb2/download) (Crown 1973, p. 23), [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/021.htm), [TP](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_28451163) and the [USC Bernards slide](https://libraries.usc.edu/sites/default/files/events/documents/Bernards%20-%20Eileen%20Chang%20Centennial%20Presentation.pdf).
- Published translation (Leo Ou-fan Lee, *Shanghai Modern*, p. 282): "I like plainness and simplicity, but I can only describe the basic human simplicity that filters through modern fashions and ideas… I don't make falsehood and truth into stark opposites. Instead, through cenci de duizhao I show truth amid the falsehood of our times, and simplicity amid the sumptuousness of superficial appearances." — via the [Bernards slide](https://libraries.usc.edu/sites/default/files/events/documents/Bernards%20-%20Eileen%20Chang%20Centennial%20Presentation.pdf)
- Variant: the opening clause reads 「我喜歡**索樸**」 in Bernards's Crown-derived slide and in [MB], but 「我喜歡**素樸**」 in [TP]. 索 may be the original reading or a recurring misprint; check against Crown.

**(i) Other lines from the same essay, useful for the page.**
- 「人是生活於一個時代裡的，可是這時代卻在影子似地沉沒下去，人覺得自己是被拋棄了。」 Matched in [TP](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_28451163) and [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/021.htm).
- 「我用這手法描寫人類在一切時代之中生活下來的記憶，而以此給予周圍的現實一個啟示。」 Matched in [TP] and [MB].
- 「我只求自己能夠寫得真實些。」 Matched in [TP] and [MB].
- The 1944 printing reads 「理論並非高高坐在上**頭**」. The later texts [TP] and [MB] read 上**面** — [SSQ](https://www.chinawriter.com.cn/n1/2020/0909/c419384-31855145.html).

### Inferences
- In this essay Chang states the humanist thesis herself, in theoretical form:
  - ordinary, "stable" life is the permanent ground ("it exists in every era");
  - the heroic or "era" mode is transient (the Superman "is born into one era");
  - weak ordinary people represent "the total" of an age better than heroes do.
  The page can lead with these lines as first-hand evidence. It does not need to rely on critics.
- The essay is also explicitly about 時代. [SSQ] counts the word 15 times in the first ~2,000 characters. So it is fairer to say Chang re-routes "the era" through ordinary lives than that she ignores it. That nuance strengthens the archive's thesis rather than weakening it.

### Gaps
- Two readings need checking against Crown 《流言》 (1968/1973/1991) or the 1944 《新東方》 scan: **不完全 vs 不安全**, and **素樸 vs 索樸**. I could not see either print text directly.
- I could not see Andrew Jones's exact English for passages (a)–(c), the 安穩/飛揚 lines. Karen Kingsbury has not, to my knowledge, published this essay. Her 2025 NYRB *Time Tunnel* (with Jie Zhang) mixes fiction and nonfiction, but I could not confirm its contents.

## Q2. 〈燼餘錄〉 "From the Ashes" (1944): the Battle of Hong Kong memoir

### Takeaway
Chang frames the 1941 siege through "irrelevant things" (不相干的事). She explicitly wishes historians would "say more irrelevant things", and says that the "zest of life" lies entirely in them. She records students fretting over clothes and food, a dying patient she failed, the "waste" of a good teacher's death, and closes on 「時代的車轟轟地往前開」. That closing image sets individual loneliness and self-absorption against the roaring vehicle of the era. All wording is verified in two traditional and simplified-derived texts, and a published English translation (Stunt, 1996) is online.

### Cited Findings

**Bibliography.** Written February 1944 ([HKU PDF](https://www.convocation.hku.hk/LUNGYingTai/doc/004.pdf), dated 「寫於1944年2月」). First published in 《天地》月刊 no. 5, February 1944 ([MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/009.htm)). Collected in 《流言》 (1944).

**English translations.**
- Oliver Stunt, "From the Ashes", *Renditions* 45 (Spring 1996) — [CUHK Renditions](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/rct/renditions/sample/b45.html)
- Andrew F. Jones, in *Written on Water* (2005); passages around p. 52 are cited — [KL](https://hkupress.hku.hk/image/catalog/pdf-preview/9789888083725.pdf)

**(a) "Irrelevant things" (opening, 42 + 44 characters). Grade V2+.**
「然而香港之戰予我的印象幾乎完全限於一些不相干的事。」
「我沒有寫歷史的志願，也沒有資格評論史家應持何種態度，可是私下裏總希望他們多說點不相干的話。」
- Matched in [HKU](https://www.convocation.hku.hk/LUNGYingTai/doc/004.pdf), [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/009.htm) and [EDB](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/tc/curriculum-development/kla/chi-edu/resources/lit-teacher-training/20251013_Clit_1.pdf).
- Published translation (Stunt): "However, my impressions of wartime Hong Kong are virtually all confined to irrelevant matters. / I have no intention of writing a history, and nor am I qualified to comment on the attitudes historians should take, but privately I have always wished they might concern themselves a little more with irrelevant matters." — [Renditions](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/rct/renditions/sample/b45.html)

**(b) Against over-tidy history, and the "struggle between small self and big self".**
「歷史如果過於注重藝術上的完整性，便成為小說了。像威爾斯的《歷史大綱》，所以不能躋於正史之列，便是因為它太合理化了一點，自始至終記述的是小我與大我的鬥爭。」
- Matched in [HKU](https://www.convocation.hku.hk/LUNGYingTai/doc/004.pdf) and [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/009.htm). [MB] misprints 路於 for 躋於.
- Stunt: "…the entire work is based on the conflict between the individual and society, and as such is just a shade over-rationalized." — [Renditions](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/rct/renditions/sample/b45.html)

**(c) The "zest of life" (44 characters). Grade V2+.**
「清堅決絕的宇宙觀，不論是政治上的還是哲學上的，總未免使人嫌煩。人生的所謂『生趣』全在那些不相干的事。」
- Matched in [HKU](https://www.convocation.hku.hk/LUNGYingTai/doc/004.pdf), [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/009.htm) and [EDB](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/tc/curriculum-development/kla/chi-edu/resources/lit-teacher-training/20251013_Clit_1.pdf).
- Stunt: "Regardless of whether they were political or philosophical, world views which [were] too clear-cut are bound to provoke antipathy. Man's joie de vivre is solely to be found in life's irrelevancies." — [Renditions](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/rct/renditions/sample/b45.html)

**(d) Students and clothes. Grade V2+.**
「在香港，我們初得到開戰的消息的時候，宿舍裏的一個女同學發起急來，道：『怎麼辦呢？沒有適當的衣服穿！』」
- Matched in [HKU](https://www.convocation.hku.hk/LUNGYingTai/doc/004.pdf) and [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/009.htm).
- 「戰爭期中各人不同的心理反應，確與衣服有關。」 Matched in [HKU] and [MB].
- Stunt: "'What am I to do? I've nothing to wear!'" and "During the war, each of our psychological responses had some close association with clothing." — [Renditions](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/rct/renditions/sample/b45.html)

**(e) Carrying on as ourselves.**
「能夠不理會的，我們一概不理會，出生入死，沉浮於最富色彩的經驗中，我們還是我們，一塵不染，維持著素日的生活典型。」
- Matched in [HKU](https://www.convocation.hku.hk/LUNGYingTai/doc/004.pdf), which prints 沉浮于, and [Renditions](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/rct/renditions/sample/b45.html), whose English matches: "we were simply what we were, unsoiled, carrying on with our ordinary routines".

**(f) The death of Professor France (N. H. France), "the waste".**
「可是他死了——最無名目的死。第一，算不了為國捐軀。即使是『光榮殉國』，又怎樣？」 … 「一個好先生，一個好人。人類的浪費……」
- Matched in [HKU](https://www.convocation.hku.hk/LUNGYingTai/doc/004.pdf) and [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/009.htm).
- This rejects national-martyrdom framing of an individual death.

**(g) Food, ice cream, and the poor (V2+).**
- 「我記得香港陷落後我們怎樣滿街的找尋冰淇淋和嘴唇膏。」 The HKU text prints 霜淇淋, which is Taiwanese usage and probably a conversion artefact.
- 「我們立在攤頭上吃滾油煎的蘿蔔餅，尺來遠腳底下就躺著窮人的青紫的屍首。」
- Both are in [HKU](https://www.convocation.hku.hk/LUNGYingTai/doc/004.pdf). Stunt: "lay the purplish corpses of the destitute" — [Renditions](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/rct/renditions/sample/b45.html)

**(h) The dying patient and "we selfish people".**
The patient with a festering wound cried 「姑娘啊！姑娘啊！」 all night, and Chang writes 「我是一個不負責任的，沒良心的看護」. On his death: 「我們這些自私的人若無其事的活下去了。」 (17 characters).
- Matched in [HKU](https://www.convocation.hku.hk/LUNGYingTai/doc/004.pdf). [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/009.htm) reads 若無其事**地**.
- Stunt: "We, the selfish, carried on living as if nothing had happened." — [Renditions](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/rct/renditions/sample/b45.html)
- Also from the hospital: 「在醫院裏，各個不同的創傷就代表了他們整個的個性。」 This is the individual, not the event, as the unit of experience.

**(i) Closing paragraph: 「時代的車轟轟地往前開」 (124 characters). Grade V2+, with one variant.**
「時代的車轟轟地往前開。我們坐在車上，經過的也許不過是幾條熟悉的街衢，可是在漫天的火光中也自驚心動魄。就可惜我們只顧忙著在一瞥即逝的店鋪的櫥窗裏找尋我們自己的影子——我們只看見自己的臉，蒼白，渺小；我們的自私與空虛，我們恬不知恥的愚蠢——誰都像我們一樣，然而我們每人都是孤獨的。」
- [HKU](https://www.convocation.hku.hk/LUNGYingTai/doc/004.pdf) reads **街衢** and 渺小：. [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/009.htm) reads **街道**, 店舖 and 渺小；.
- Stunt's "familiar thoroughfare" suggests his source text had 街衢.
- Taiwan's 國立臺灣文學館 exhibit quotes the opening as 「時代的車轟轟的往前開，我們坐在車上……」, with 的 for 地 — [vocus exhibit report](https://vocus.cc/article/62dba44efd89780001e8e43b), via a search snippet only.
- Published translation (Stunt), which renders 時代 as "life": "And the vehicle of life rumbles on. As we travel on it, we may go no further afield than the odd familiar thoroughfare, but the glow of flaming skies will still stir the soul. More's the pity that we preoccupy ourselves with searching for a reflection of ourselves in a passing shop window - only to see faces, our own faces, pallid, paltry. In our self-centre vacuity, in our smug, shameless stupidity, we all resemble each other. And each of us remains alone." — [Renditions](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/rct/renditions/sample/b45.html)
- Fresh literal rendering of the first sentence: "The vehicle of the times rumbles forward."

**(j) An unflattering line that complicates the thesis.**
「去掉了一切的浮文，剩下的仿佛只有飲食男女這兩項。」 Matched in [HKU](https://www.convocation.hku.hk/LUNGYingTai/doc/004.pdf), [EDB](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/tc/curriculum-development/kla/chi-edu/resources/lit-teacher-training/20251013_Clit_1.pdf) and Stunt's "two things alone appear to remain: food and sex".

### Inferences
- 〈燼餘錄〉 is the strongest evidence that Chang's attention to ordinary people was **unsentimental**. She indicts herself and her classmates as "selfish", "shameless" and "stupid". Her compassion is clear-eyed rather than idealising. The page should present it that way, so as not to overclaim "care".
- The essay's method is close to a programme for an "archive of the irrelevant". Asking historians to "say more irrelevant things" fits an archive page about small, individual lives.

### Gaps
- I could not see Jones's 2005 translation wording for any passage.
- Whether the Crown 《流言》 reads 街衢 or 街道 is unconfirmed. Stunt's "thoroughfare" and the HKU text favour 街衢.

## Q3. 〈傾城之戀〉 "Love in a Fallen City" (1943): the ending

### Takeaway
The ending explicitly subordinates the historical event to one woman's private fate. It says, ironically, that perhaps a whole city fell to "complete" her, and that she did not feel her "place in history" was anything special. Just before this, it says that in this chaotic era "there is no room for individualists, but there is always room for an ordinary married couple." All sentences are verified in two or more texts. Kingsbury's published English of the 陷落 passage is verified via [KL].

### Cited Findings

**Bibliography.** 1943; collected in 《傳奇》 (1944), which Chang described as stories written for Shanghai readers ([MB 〈到底是上海人〉](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/002.htm)). Crown edition: 《傾城之戀：張愛玲短篇小說集之一》 (1991, 2010) ([cksh handout](https://www.cksh.tp.edu.tw/wp-content/uploads/doc/cg255/傾城之戀-李明照老師.pdf); [smgsh](https://lib.smgsh.tc.edu.tw/userfiles/李盈瑩(2).pdf)). The often-cited first serialisation in 《雜誌》 (September–October 1943) was not re-verified here.

**(a) "A selfish man, a selfish woman… an ordinary couple" (58 characters). Grade V2+.**
「他不過是一個自私的男子，她不過是一個自私的女人。在這兵荒馬亂的時代，個人主義者是無處容身的，可是總有地方容得下一對平凡的夫妻。」
- Matched in a Taipei reader's quotation of the Crown edition ([vocus 天心居](https://vocus.cc/article/65436152fd89780001dade29)), [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/000/026.htm) (whose OCR drops the first 他) and [163.com](https://www.163.com/dy/article/L3IPPMR30556O3YK.html), which is simplified.
- The preceding sentence is 「他們把彼此看得透明透亮，僅僅是一剎那的徹底的諒解，然而這一剎那夠他們在一起和諧地活個十年八年。」 [MB] has a comma after 透亮; [vocus] has a full stop.
- Fresh rendering: "He was only a selfish man; she was only a selfish woman. In this age of soldiers and chaos there is no place for an individualist, but there is always room for an ordinary husband and wife."

**(b) 「香港的陷落成全了她」 (102 characters). Grade V2+.**
「香港的陷落成全了她。但是在這不可理喻的世界裡，誰知道什麼是因，什麼是果？誰知道呢，也許就因為要成全她，一個大都市傾覆了。成千上萬的人死去，成千上萬的人痛苦著，跟著是驚天動地的大改革……流蘇並不覺得她在歷史上的地位有什麼微妙之點。她只是笑盈盈地站起身來，將蚊煙香盤踢到桌子底下去。」
- Matched in [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/000/026.htm) and [EDB](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/tc/curriculum-development/kla/chi-edu/resources/lit-teacher-training/20251013_Clit_1.pdf). The EDB text gives the first three sentences and punctuates 「誰知道呢？也許…」 with a question mark.
- The last sentence (笑盈盈地… 踢到桌子底下去) was seen only in [MB]. It is V1; check it against Crown before quoting.
- **Published translation (Karen S. Kingsbury, *Love in a Fallen City*, NYRB 2007, p. 167):** "Hong Kong's defeat had brought Liusu victory. But in this unreasonable world, who can distinguish cause and effect? Who knows which is which? Did a great city fall so that she could be vindicated? Countless thousands of people dead, countless thousands of people suffering, after that an earthshaking revolution… Liusu did not feel anything subtle about her place in history." — quoted in full with citation in [KL, ch. notes](https://hkupress.hku.hk/image/catalog/pdf-preview/9789888083725.pdf)

**(c) Final lines (68 characters). Grade V2+.**
「傳奇裡的傾城傾國的人大抵如此。到處都是傳奇，可不見得有這麼圓滿的收場。胡琴咿咿呀呀拉著，在萬盞燈火的夜晚，拉過來又拉過去，說不盡的蒼涼的故事——不問也罷！」
- Matched in [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/000/026.htm), whose OCR drops 到 from 到處, and the [WashU digital exhibit](https://digitalexhibits.library.washu.edu/s/love-and-desires/page/love-in-a-fallen-city). The exhibit is simplified and reads 咿咿呀呀的拉着.
- One search summary wrongly placed 「到處都是傳奇」 in the opening paragraph. It is the closing.
- I could not see Kingsbury's English for these lines. Fresh rendering: "The people in legends who toppled cities and kingdoms were probably all like this. There are legends everywhere, but they don't necessarily end so happily. The huqin wails on, in a night of ten thousand lamps, drawn back and forth, telling an endless desolate story — better not to ask!"

**(d) Chang's own gloss on these characters,** in 〈自己的文章〉: 流蘇 was not "transformed into a revolutionary woman" by the war; 柳原 turned to "a plain, practical life". 「因之柳原與流蘇的結局，雖然多少是健康的，仍舊是庸俗；就事論事，他們也只能如此。」 Matched in [TP](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_28451163) and [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/021.htm).

### Inferences
- The ending is double-edged. It privileges a private life over the fall of a city, but with irony: "selfish", "庸俗", a city falling "to complete her". The thesis is better put as "Chang refuses to let the national catastrophe swallow the individual" than as "Chang celebrates the individual".
- 「個人主義者是無處容身的，可是總有地方容得下一對平凡的夫妻」 is perhaps the single best line for the archive's thesis about ordinary lives outlasting history.

### Gaps
- Kingsbury's translation of 「到處都是傳奇…」 and of the "selfish man/woman" sentence was not seen.
- No direct Crown print check was possible for the 笑盈盈地 sentence.

## Q4. Other essays: ordinary people, 小市民 and everyday life (verified only)

### Takeaway
Six further essays contain clear first-hand statements that can be verified. 〈談女人〉 is especially valuable because it is **Chang's own, first-hand use of 慈悲**, unlike the Hu Lancheng-sourced 「因為懂得，所以慈悲」. Most of these passages are verified in only one full text ([MB]), plus a scholarly quotation in a few cases. Grades are marked one by one.

### Cited Findings

**〈必也正名乎〉** ("Rectification of Names"; first published January 1944, venue garbled in [MB]'s note; Crown 《流言》 1991, pp. 35–40). **Grade V2+**, from Crown via [ZXH](https://thjcs.site.nthu.edu.tw/var/file/452/1452/img/4082/329626054.pdf) and from [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/008.htm). 77 characters:
「可是世上有用的人往往是俗人。我願意保留我的俗不可耐的名字，向我自己作為一種警告，設法除去一般知書識字的人咬文嚼字的積習，從柴米油鹽、肥皂、水與太陽之中去找尋實際的人生。」
- [ZXH] quotes the second sentence verbatim from Crown and glosses it as Chang's 「親近日常生活的自我提醒」 ("a self-reminder to stay close to daily life").
- Fresh rendering: "…yet the useful people in the world are often vulgar, common people. I want to keep my unbearably vulgar name as a warning to myself: to rid myself of the lettered person's habit of chewing over words, and to look for real life among firewood, rice, oil and salt, soap, water and sunlight."

**〈談女人〉** ("On Women", 1944; in 《流言》). **Grade V1**, [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/000/009.htm) only. 48 characters:
「超人是男性的，神卻帶有女性的成分。超人與神不同。超人是進取的，是一種生存的目標。神是廣大的同情，慈悲，了解，安息。」
- Further on: 「女人縱有千般不是，女人的精神裡面卻有一點『地母』的根芽。」 This draws on O'Neill's Earth Mother in *The Great God Brown*.
- These lines exactly parallel 〈自己的文章〉's 「超人…人的神性，也可以說是婦人性」.
- Fresh rendering: "The Superman is male; the divine has something female in it. The Superman and God are different. The Superman is aggressive, a goal of existence. God is vast sympathy, compassion, understanding, repose."

**〈寫什麼〉** ("What to Write"; first published in 《雜誌》 vol. 13 no. 5, August 1944, per [MB]). **Grade V1**, [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/024.htm).
- The essay opens with a friend asking 「無產階級的故事你會寫麼？」. Chang answers that she knows only a little about 「阿媽她們的事」.
- 「文人只須老老實實生活著，然後，如果他是個文人，他自然會把他想到的一切寫出來。他寫所能夠寫的，無所謂應當。」 (46 characters)
- 「只要題材不太專門性，像戀愛結婚，生老病死，這一類頗為普遍的現象，都可以從無數各各不同的觀點來寫，一輩子也寫不完。」 (50 characters)
- This is a direct refusal of assigned "correct" subject matter (應當).

**〈《傳奇》再版的話〉** (preface to the second printing of *Romances*, dated 民國三十三年九月十四日 = 14 September 1944). **Grade V1** for the Chinese wording, [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/030.htm). 78 characters:
「個人即使等得及，時代是倉促的，已經在破壞中，還有更大的破壞要來。有一天我們的文明，不論是昇華還是浮華，都要成為過去。如果我最常用的字是『荒涼』，那是因為思想背景裡有這惘惘的威脅。」
- Also 「出名要趁早呀！」 — genuine, from the same preface ([MB]).
- A Kingsbury and Chang English translation exists: "Preface to the Second Printing of *Romances*", in *Love in a Fallen City and Other Stories* (Penguin, 2007), p. 2 — [KL](https://hkupress.hku.hk/image/catalog/pdf-preview/9789888083725.pdf), note 51. I could not see its wording.

**〈中國的日夜〉** ("Days and Nights of China"; epilogue to 《傳奇》增訂本, Shanghai 山河圖書公司, November 1946 — [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/041.htm); epilogue status confirmed in [KL](https://hkupress.hku.hk/image/catalog/pdf-preview/9789888083725.pdf), note 15). **Grade V1** for wording.
- 「我真快樂我是走在中國的太陽底下。」 (15 characters). Said while carrying the day's groceries home.
- From the poem (65 characters for these lines):
  「我的路／走在我自己的國土。／亂紛紛都是自己人；／補了又補，連了又連的／補釘的彩雲的人民。／我的人民，／我的青春。／我真高興曬著太陽去買回來／沉重累贅的一日三餐。」
- Prose: 「我們中國本來是補釘的國家，連天都是女媧補過的。」 [MB] misprints 女娟.
- Jones's translation is in *Written on Water* — [KL](https://hkupress.hku.hk/image/catalog/pdf-preview/9789888083725.pdf), notes 45 and 50, "Epilogue: Days and Nights of China," pp. 214–215.
- This is Chang's one sustained affirmation of "China". Note that it is built from patched clothes, street vendors and groceries, not the state. "My people" is patched, chaotic and ordinary.

**〈公寓生活記趣〉** ("Notes on Apartment Life"; 《天地》 no. 3, December 1943, per [MB]). **Grade V1**, [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/005.htm).
「我喜歡聽市聲。比我較有詩意的人在枕上聽松濤，聽海嘯，我是非得聽見電車響才睡得著覺的。」
- [MB] misprints 待意. "詩意" is my emendation and must be checked.

**〈到底是上海人〉** ("Shanghainese, After All"; 《雜誌》 vol. 11 no. 5, August 1943, per [MB]). **Grade V1**, [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/002.htm).
- 「誰都說上海人壞，可是壞得有分寸。」
- 「因此我寫的故事裡沒有一個主角是個『完人』。」
- 「我喜歡上海人，我希望上海人喜歡我的書。」
- [MB] also says she 「為上海人寫了一本香港傳奇」.

**〈我看蘇青〉** ("My View of Su Qing"; 《天地》 no. 19, April 1945, per [MB]). **Grade V1** for the full wording, from [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/038.htm). 89 characters:
「我想到許多人的命運，連我在內的；有一種鬱鬱蒼蒼的身世之感。『身世之感』普通總是自傷、自憐的意思罷，但我想是可以有更廣大的解釋的。將來的平安，來到的時候已經不是我們的了，我們只能各人就近求得自己的平安。」
- [Xinmin Weekly](https://m.xinminweekly.com.cn/content/17290.html) (state-affiliated; wording only) quotes the first two sentences with 「更大的解釋」 rather than 更廣大. That is a variant; check against Crown 《餘韻》/《流言》.

**Genuine but outside scope:** 「生命是一襲華美的袍，爬滿了蚤子。」 from 〈天才夢〉 (1939/1940, 西風). [MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/001.htm) has it, with an OCR error 飽.

**Gloss on 小市民 (secondary only).** Géraldine Fiss (USC) calls Chang a chronicler "not of political issues of the day, but of stories of love and everyday life, particularly the everyday life of middle-class 'petty urbanite' (xiao shimin 小市民) women" — [USC/Fiss](https://libraries.usc.edu/sites/default/files/events/documents/Ge%CC%81raldine%20Fiss%20Response%20to%20Presentations_v2.pdf). This is a scholar's description, **not** Chang's words. I did not find a verified sentence in which Chang herself uses 小市民 as programme.

**Hu Lancheng on Chang and 小市民 (first-hand to Hu, secondhand as evidence about Chang).** From 《今生今世·民國女子》: 「愛玲自己便是愛描寫民國世界小奸小壞的市民」 — [thepaper re-upload of sections 7–10](https://m.thepaper.cn/baijiahao_20542913) (state outlet; wording only). The same excerpt records Chang saying 「現代的東西縱有千般不是，它到底是我們的，與我們親。」 That is Chang's speech as reported by Hu.

### Inferences
- These essays show the same poetics across 1943–45: the useful, real and "divine" lie in 柴米油鹽, street noise, groceries and patched clothes. They are not in "monuments of the era" or 應當 subjects.
- 〈談女人〉's 「神是廣大的同情，慈悲，了解，安息」 lets the page anchor "compassion" in Chang's own text rather than in the Hu-sourced slogan.

### Gaps
- Only [MB] was available for 〈談女人〉, 〈寫什麼〉, 〈中國的日夜〉, 〈公寓生活記趣〉, 〈到底是上海人〉, 〈再版的話〉 and 〈我看蘇青〉. Each needs one more independent check, ideally Crown 《流言》/《傳奇》/《餘韻》, before quoting on the live page.
- 〈私語〉 and 《流言》's front matter were not mined for statements on ordinary life.
- 《流言》 has no prose preface as such. The title echoes Keats's "writ in water" (Nicole Huang's introduction, via [USC/Fiss](https://libraries.usc.edu/sites/default/files/events/documents/Ge%CC%81raldine%20Fiss%20Response%20to%20Presentations_v2.pdf)).

## Q5. 「因為懂得，所以慈悲」 and 「低到塵埃裡…開出花來」: true provenance; spurious attributions

### Takeaway
Both famous lines are known **only from Hu Lancheng's memoir 《今生今世》**, in the chapter 〈民國女子〉 (first published 1958–59 in Japan; later Taipei editions, e.g. 遠景 1976/1997). Neither appears in any of Chang's own writings. No original letter or photograph has been produced. So the attribution to Chang is **second-hand, via Hu**, and should be labelled "as recorded by Hu Lancheng". I could not view the primary 〈民國女子〉 text. **The exact wording and context below are UNVERIFIED against Hu's text.** A great many popular "Zhang Ailing quotes" are outright fakes, listed below.

### Cited Findings

**What the secondary sources say about 懂得/慈悲.** All these are secondary, and several are state-affiliated, so they are used for the claims only:
- After their first meetings, Hu wrote Chang a poem and a letter about her person and writing, and 「愛玲的回信是：因為懂得，所以慈悲。」 — [Sina Culture, 2004](http://news.sina.com.cn/cul/2004-11-08/312.html)
- Miao Wei 苗煒 in [Xinmin Weekly, 2022](https://m.xinminweekly.com.cn/content/17290.html) (state-affiliated) gives the same sequence: Hu wrote to Chang, and Chang replied 「因為懂得，所以慈悲」. He adds that all of this, and the photo inscription, rests on Hu's 〈民國女子〉 alone, a 「孤證」 (single uncorroborated witness). He also reports that Chang later wrote to C. T. Hsia that the parts of Hu's book about her were 「纏夾得奇怪」.
- A Tencent News explainer of fake Chang quotes states flatly: 「應是『因為懂得，所以慈悲』，出自胡蘭成《今生今世》；張愛玲自己的文章沒寫過這兩句話。」 — [QQ News, 2021](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20210912A0020600)
- People's Daily's 人民周刊 (state; claim only) adds a pairing, Hu's 「因為相知，所以懂得」 with Chang's 「因為懂得，所以慈悲」 — [人民周刊 2016](https://paper.people.com.cn/rmzk/html/2016-02/26/content_1656984.htm). I could not trace a primary source for Hu's half; treat it as UNVERIFIED.
- The precise context is disputed, and I could not check it. A Zhihu answer (via a search snippet only) says Hu had written that she was 謙遜 ("modest") and she replied with the line. Treat as UNVERIFIED.

**What the secondary sources say about 塵埃.**
- Hu says Chang gave him the photo of herself that had appeared in 《天地》 and inscribed on the back: 「見了他，她變得很低很低，低到塵埃裡，但她心裡是歡喜的，從塵埃裡開出花來。」 — [Sina 2004](http://news.sina.com.cn/cul/2004-11-08/312.html); [Xinmin Weekly](https://m.xinminweekly.com.cn/content/17290.html)
- 人民周刊 punctuates it 「…低到塵埃裡。但她心裡是歡喜的…」 — [人民周刊](https://paper.people.com.cn/rmzk/html/2016-02/26/content_1656984.htm). This punctuation variant is unresolved.
- Hu reports the inscription; there is no surviving photo-back. Xinmin: 「照片後面寫沒寫這段話，完全是胡蘭成《民國女子》這篇文章裡自己說的」.
- The inscription speaks of "she" in the third person. This is consistent with Hu's account but proves nothing.

**「現世安穩，歲月靜好」.** Their 1944 marriage document. Hu himself says these eight characters were **added by him**, per [Xinmin Weekly](https://m.xinminweekly.com.cn/content/17290.html). They are therefore not Chang's words, although they are routinely attributed to her.

**A modern forgery in circulation.** A long, sentimental "letter from Hu Lancheng to Zhang Ailing", headed 「因為懂得，所以慈悲（胡蘭成寫給張愛玲的情書）」, was posted in 2014 by a Douban user and reposted on thepaper's user platform. It is **clearly a modern pastiche**: it recounts events up to Chang returning his letters and quotes 炎櫻 in modern idiom. In it "Hu" claims to have written the line to her — [Douban 2014](https://www.douban.com/group/topic/49366596/); [thepaper 澎湃號 repost](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_2745941). Do not cite it.

**Common fake or misattributed "Chang quotes",** from the Tencent explainer's images, which I viewed — [QQ News 2021](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20210912A0020600):
- 「因為愛過，所以慈悲；因為懂得，所以寬容。」 A corruption of the Hu-sourced line. It is listed as genuine Chang on **Chinese Wikiquote**, unsourced — [zh.wikiquote 張愛玲](https://zh.wikiquote.org/wiki/%E5%BC%B5%E6%84%9B%E7%8E%B2). Wikiquote is unreliable here.
- 「你還不來，我怎敢老去。」, 「人生太長，我們怕寂寞；人生太短，我們怕來不及」, 「海上月是天上月，眼前人是心上人」, 「向來心是看客心，奈何人是劇中人」. These belong to the same fake cluster.
- 「相逢，不是恨晚，便是恨早。」 The explainer attributes it to 張小嫻.
- 「時間無涯，剛好趕上」. A rewrite of Chang's genuine 〈愛〉 (1944): 「於千萬人之中遇見你所要遇見的人，於千萬年之中，時間的無涯的荒野裡，沒有早一步，也沒有晚一步，剛巧趕上了…」
- The 「飯渣子…朱砂痣」 paraphrase is often credited to 《半生緣》. The genuine source is 〈紅玫瑰與白玫瑰〉 and its own wording.
- 「我們都是戲子，在別人的故事裡，流著自己的眼淚」. Adapted from 席慕蓉's poem 〈戲子〉.
- 「愛情本來並不複雜，來來去去不過三個字…」. From 張小嫻.
- 「有些人一直沒機會見…」 (〈有些人，有些事，有些話，有些愛〉). An anonymous internet piece.
- 「每個人心裡都有一座傷城」, 「生命是一朵千瓣蓮花…」, 「我以為愛情可以填滿人生的遺憾…」. Listed as fake internet sayings.

**Genuine lines often doubted:**
- 「笑，全世界便與你同聲笑；哭，你便獨自哭。」 Genuine in 〈花凋〉, where it is quoted as two lines seen in classmates' autograph albums, after Ella Wheeler Wilcox — [MB 〈花凋〉](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/000/005.htm).
- 「出名要趁早」. Genuine, from 〈《傳奇》再版的話〉 ([MB](http://www.millionbook.net/mj/z/zhangailing/zalj/030.htm)).

**Chinese Wikiquote also lists, unsourced:** 「不管你的條件有多差，總會有個人在愛你…」, 「人生很短，一轉身就是一輩子」, 「我相信人，但我不相信人性」, 「長的是磨難，短的是人生」, 「年輕是嗎？別着急，會老的」 and 「如果你認識從前的我，也許你會原諒現在的我」 — [zh.wikiquote](https://zh.wikiquote.org/wiki/%E5%BC%B5%E6%84%9B%E7%8E%B2). I found no source for any of these in Chang's works, so all are **UNVERIFIED** and probably spurious. The last one is widely said to come from a photo or letter but has no traceable source.

**Hu's own contrary characterisation.** Per a Douban long-read by 戴文采, Hu writes in 《今生今世》 that Chang was 「慈悲布施全無，非常自私，臨事心狠手辣」 — [Douban](https://www.douban.com/group/topic/7801340/). This is UNVERIFIED against Hu's text, but it is a pointed irony beside the 慈悲 slogan.

### Inferences
- For an archive page on Chang's "compassion", 「因為懂得，所以慈悲」 should appear only with the explicit attribution "Chang's reply as recorded by Hu Lancheng in 《今生今世》 (1959); no independent record". The first-hand anchor should be 〈談女人〉's 「神是廣大的同情，慈悲，了解，安息」 (Q4), with 〈自己的文章〉's 「人的神性，也可以說是婦人性」 (Q1).
- Hu was a Wang Jingwei-regime propagandist writing a self-flattering memoir. Chang called his account of her 「纏夾得奇怪」, as relayed by Xinmin. So even the 塵埃 inscription should carry that caveat.

### Gaps
- **The exact text of Hu's 〈民國女子〉 passages was not seen.** An Internet Archive scan exists, 《今生今世》 (Taipei: 遠景, 1997), `jinshengjinshi008800`, but it is lending-restricted and search-inside failed. A library check of the 遠景 or 三三 edition is needed for exact wording and page numbers, including whether the reply concerned 謙遜.
- The Chang–Hsia letter containing 「纏夾得奇怪」 was not located. It should be in 夏志清, 《張愛玲給我的信件》 (聯合文學, 1997) or the 2013 《張愛玲往來書信集》. This is UNVERIFIED first-hand.
- It was not checked whether Chang's own 《小團圓》 (2009) contains any version of either line.

## Q6. Chang's own English-language statements about writing ordinary people rather than politics

### Takeaway
I found only two short first-hand English sentences. Both come from Chang's autobiographical entry in *World Authors 1950–1970* (ed. John Wakeman, H. W. Wilson, 1975), and both are attested **only** via a China Daily article (PRC state media; wording only). They show Chang resisting the Western convention of "Chinese as Confucian aphorists" and publishers' demand for sympathetic poor characters. Neither is a direct statement of "ordinary people over politics". The primary volume should be checked before use.

### Cited Findings
- "The publishers here seem to agree that the characters in those two novels are too unpleasant, even that the poor are no better" — Chang in *World Authors 1950–1970* (1975), about her rejected autobiographical novels *The Fall of the Pagoda* and *The Book of Change* (completed c. 1963). Quoted by Chitralekha Basu and Mei Jia, [China Daily European Weekly, 30 September 2011](https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/201109/30/WS5a29f293a3101a51ddf8de94.html). This is state media and the only source.
- "Here I came against the curious literary convention treating the Chinese as a nation of Confucian philosophers spouting aphorisms, an anomaly in modern literature." — same entry, same single source ([China Daily](https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/201109/30/WS5a29f293a3101a51ddf8de94.html)).
- A related, verified English-language statement by Karen Kingsbury, the translator, not Chang: Chang's fiction was "not just something about Chinese-ness, or about one huge people's modern-day history of struggle and change, but about mental and social life in general." — [AAWW, Shu-Ling Chua](https://aaww.org/a-lifelong-romance-reflections-on-eileen-changs-life-work-and-legacy/)

### Inferences
- "even that the poor are no better" fits the 〈燼餘錄〉 stance: Chang refused to idealise the poor or the "people". Her attention to ordinary lives is not populist sentimentality.

### Gaps
- The *World Authors 1950–1970* entry itself was not seen, so its page numbers are unknown. Its full text may contain more relevant statements.
- I did not find English-language interviews, or English letters (to Hsia, Ferdinand Reyher, the Soongs, Richard McCarthy), in which Chang discusses ordinary people versus politics. Her 1950s USIS-sponsored anti-Communist novels (*The Rice-Sprout Song*, *Naked Earth*) are a complicating counter-example: those are political commissions. A separate researcher should check her English prefaces and letters, e.g. Kao Chuan-chih (高全之), 《張愛玲學》, and the 2020 《張愛玲往來書信集》.
- The Google Books API quota was exhausted, so no snippet-level checks of Jones's *Written on Water* or Kingsbury's *Love in a Fallen City* English could be made beyond the citations reproduced in [KL].
