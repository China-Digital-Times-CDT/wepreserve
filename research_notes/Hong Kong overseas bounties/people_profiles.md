# Hong Kong overseas national-security bounties: who the wanted people are

Research date: 2026-10-20. The newest dated item found is Zhang Xinyan reaching Vancouver on 3 Aug 2026 (HKFP, 6 Aug 2026), followed by an HKFP crackdown explainer dated 9 Aug 2026. I found nothing newer about bounties, so treat the notes as current to **about August 2026**.

Source notes:
- HKFP, the Guardian, CNN, AP and Reuters pages often block the research crawler (HTTP 429, 403 or 451). Several quotes were therefore read through syndicated copies: AP via CBC, Reuters via the Himalayan Times, CNN via KRDO, SMH via a repost, AFP via Free Malaysia Today. The original outlet is named in each case.
- I avoided PRC state media. Every charge below is the **Hong Kong government's allegation**. None has been tested in court in the person's presence.
- Safety: I give no home towns or addresses (town names in some reports were left out on purpose), no birthdates, and nothing about relatives beyond the general pattern noted once.

## Q1. The 19 people named in the 2023–2024 bounty rounds: profiles and Chinese-name check

### Takeaway
All 19 names on the user's list were confirmed in the official rounds of 3 Jul 2023 (8 people), 14 Dec 2023 (5) and 24 Dec 2024 (6), each with a HK$1 million bounty. **Three Chinese names on the user's list are wrong:** Johnny Fok is **霍嘉誌** (not 霍嘉志), Carmen Lau is **劉珈汶** (not 劉凱文), and Chloe Cheung is **張晞晴** (not 張曉雯). The other 16 Chinese names are correct.

### Round 1: 3 July 2023

**Nathan Law 羅冠聰 (Law Kwun-chung)**
- **Before leaving:** Student leader in the 2014 Umbrella Movement and secretary-general of the HK Federation of Students (2015–16). Co-founded Demosistō and chaired it (2016). In Sept 2016, at 23, he became the youngest person ever elected to LegCo; he was disqualified in July 2017 over his oath. He was jailed in 2017 over the 2014 Civic Square protest, and the Court of Final Appeal overturned the prison term in 2018 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Law); [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/world/china-hong-kong-democracy-activists-dissidents-bounty-nathan-law-rcna92512)
- **Leaving:** Left on 2 July 2020, the day after the NSL took effect, citing safety, and settled in London. He announced UK asylum on 7 April 2021 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Law)
- **Work abroad:** Testified to the US Congress by video in July 2020. Named to the TIME100 in 2020 ([TIME](https://time.com/collection/100-most-influential-people-2020/5888201/nathan-law/)). Pritzker Fellow at the University of Chicago Institute of Politics (2021). Books: *Freedom: How We Lose It and How We Fight Back* (2021) and *When the Wind Blows* (2024) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Law)
- **Later measures:** HK passport revoked in June 2024 under the Safeguarding National Security Ordinance (SNSO) — [HKFP 12 Jun 2024](https://hongkongfp.com/2024/06/12/breaking-hong-kong-cancels-passports-of-6-wanted-activists-in-uk-inc-nathan-law-under-new-security-law-provision/). Singapore refused him entry in Sept 2025, saying his presence "would not be in Singapore's national interests" — [CNA](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/singapore/hong-kong-activist-nathan-law-denied-entry-national-interests-5374966)
- **HK government allegation:** incitement to secession and collusion with foreign forces — [zh.wikipedia list](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-tw/%E9%A6%99%E6%B8%AF%E5%9C%8B%E5%AE%B6%E5%AE%89%E5%85%A8%E6%A1%88%E4%BB%B6%E9%80%9A%E7%B7%9D%E4%BA%BA%E5%A3%AB%E5%88%97%E8%A1%A8)

**Dennis Kwok 郭榮鏗 (Kwok Wing-hang)**

- **Leaving:** Announced his exit from politics on 21 Nov 2020 and left later that month. Now in the US — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dennis_Kwok)

Co-founded the US law firm Elliott Kwok Levine & Jaroslaw LLP in 2022 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dennis_Kwok). The NYC Bar (Aug 2023) and the NY State Bar (Sept 2023) both condemned the warrant against him — [NYC Bar PDF](https://www.nycbar.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Hong_Kong_Bounties.pdf); [NYSBA](https://nysba.org/?p=143552)

**Ted Hui 許智峯 (Hui Chi-fung)**

- **Leaving:** Arrested in Aug 2020 over a 2019 protest. Left via Denmark in Dec 2020, went to the UK, then moved to Australia in March 2021 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_Hui)

HK passport cancelled on 24 Dec 2024 — [CBC/AP](https://www.cbc.ca/news/world/hong-kong-arrest-warrants-bounties-activists-1.7418476)

The office of Foreign Minister Penny Wong said Australia "will not tolerate" surveillance, harassment or intimidation — [Malay Mail/Reuters, 18 Mar 2025](https://www.malaymail.com/news/world/2025/03/18/australian-govt-denounces-hong-kong-linked-reward-for-activist-kevin-yam-vows-to-protect-citizens/169989); [Sen. **Elmer Yuen 袁弓夷 (Yuen Gong-yi)**

### Round 3: 24 December 2024

**Tony Chung 鍾翰林 (Chung Hon-lam)**
- **Before leaving:** Co-founded and led **Studentlocalism** (2016 to June 2020). Arrested outside the US Consulate in Oct 2020, allegedly while trying to seek asylum. - **Work abroad:** Has led London protests against Article 23 and for closing the HK Economic and Trade Office (HKETO) in London — [HKFP explainer](https://hongkongfp.com/2024/12/28/explainer-who-are-the-six-overseas-activists-facing-new-hong-kong-nat-security-arrest-warrants/)
- **HK government allegation:** incitement to secession and collusion — [zh.wikipedia list](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-tw/%E9%A6%99%E6%B8%AF%E5%9C%8B%E5%AE%B6%E5%AE%89%E5%85%A8%E6%A1%88%E4%BB%B6%E9%80%9A%E7%B7%9D%E4%BA%BA%E5%A3%AB%E5%88%97%E8%A1%A8). ## Q2. Later rounds: the 2025 "Hong Kong Parliament" warrants, and any 2026 rounds

### Takeaway
On 25 Jul 2025 police named 19 people over the overseas "Hong Kong Parliament". Four already carried HK$1M bounties, and **15 were new, with HK$200,000 each**. That brought the overseas total to **34**. I found **no new bounty round from August 2025 through about August 2026**. Later actions were SNSO "absconder" measures (4 Aug 2025), a ban on the group (Nov 2025), and the Thailand detention of Zhang Xinyan (2026).

### Cited Findings
- The official release splits the 19 into nine organisers ("organised, outside the HKSAR, election") and ten "candidates… [who] upon being elected, took an oath to serve as so-called 'members of the Hong Kong Parliament'". Nearly every person who responded publicly rejected the bounty as intimidation and pledged to continue. Several used irony, calling it an "honour", "hilarious" or a "Christmas present". Verbatim quotes exist for most; Dennis Kwok, Johnny Fok, Tony Choi and Joe Tay (verbatim) are gaps.
